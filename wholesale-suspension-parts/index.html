What we stock for this range

Control arms, ball joints, tie rod ends, stabilizer links, bushings and shock absorbers are the volume lines. We supply complete control arm kits with bushings and ball joints pre-fitted, which saves your workshop labour time and removes the risk of a mismatched bushing. Wheel hub bearing assemblies are a separate high-value line with steady demand.

Buying note: Always order control arms in axle pairs. Fitting one side only is the single most common cause of a premature failure on the other side, and it is the most common warranty complaint in this category.
