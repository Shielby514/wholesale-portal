What we stock for this range

A working brake container usually mixes pads, discs, brake shoes and hydraulic components. Pads and discs are bought together; calipers, master cylinders and wheel cylinders are replacement items that move more slowly but at far higher unit value. We stock both economy-grade and OEM-equivalent grades and will not ship a grade you did not ask for.

Buying note: Brake pads are graded by friction coefficient (FF, FG, GG). For hot-climate and mountainous markets, quote the GG grade — economy pads glaze and squeal within months in those conditions.

Common questions

What is the minimum order quantity for brake parts?
No minimum per part number. Most buyers build a mixed list of 40-100 numbers instead of a large quantity of a single number.
Which brands do you cover for brake parts?

Do you supply OEM-grade brake parts or aftermarket?
Both. We quote OEM-equivalent and economy grades separately so you can choose the price point that fits your market — we never substitute a grade you did not order.
What is the lead time for brake parts?
Stock items ship in 7-12 days. Consolidated containers take 25-35 days to FOB Nansha plus transit.

Yes. Consolidation across categories is how most of our containers are built and it is the main reason buyers use us rather than several separate suppliers.
