What we stock for this range

Timing chains and belts, tensioners, gasket sets, water and oil pumps, pistons, crankshafts and cylinder head assemblies make up most of our engine parts shipments. Timing components should always ship as complete kits — a chain without its tensioner and guides will fail early and take the engine with it.

Buying note: Timing chain jobs must be quoted as complete kits including guides, tensioner and sprockets. We will not split a timing kit, and neither should you — partial kits are the number one cause of engine warranty claims.
