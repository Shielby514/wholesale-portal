What we stock for this range

Fuel pumps, injectors, fuel filters, fuel rails, fuel tank caps and sender units. Diesel injectors and high-pressure fuel pumps are the highest-value items and the most specification-sensitive — common-rail components must be matched to the exact engine code.

Buying note: Common-rail injectors are coded to the engine. Order by VIN, not by model year, and never substitute a "similar" injector — the flow rate difference will damage the engine.
