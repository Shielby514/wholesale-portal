What we stock for this range

Alternators, starters, ignition coils, sensors, switches, window regulators and lighting assemblies are the volume lines. Sensors in particular — oxygen sensors, MAF sensors, crankshaft position sensors, ABS wheel speed sensors — are high-turnover and individually packaged items that suit mixed containers well.

Buying note: Electrical parts are non-returnable once fitted. Confirm the OE number and the VIN before ordering, and expect to keep a small buffer stock of sensors rather than ordering per job.

Common questions

What is the minimum order quantity for electrical parts?
No minimum per part number. Most buyers build a mixed list of 40-100 numbers instead of a large quantity of a single number.
Which brands do you cover for electrical parts?
Hyundai, Kia, Mercedes-Benz, BMW, Audi, Volkswagen and Toyota are the deepest, and we add new applications to the catalogue every week.
Do you supply OEM-grade electrical parts or aftermarket?
Both. We quote OEM-equivalent and economy grades separately so you can choose the price point that fits your market — we never substitute a grade you did not order.
What is the lead time for electrical parts?
Stock items ship in 7-12 days. Consolidated containers take 25-35 days to FOB Nansha plus transit.
Can electrical parts be consolidated with other categories?
Yes. Consolidation across categories is how most of our containers are built and it is the main reason buyers use us rather than several separate suppliers.
