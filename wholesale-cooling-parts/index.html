What we stock for this range

Radiators, condensers, evaporators, thermostats, water pumps, expansion tanks and cooling fans make up this category. Radiators for hot-climate markets should be specified with increased core thickness, which we can supply on request for container quantities. Air conditioning condensers and evaporators are a growing line as more markets demand working A/C as standard.

Buying note: For Gulf, West African and Central Asian markets, order the heavy-duty core radiator. Standard cores are the most returned cooling part in those regions and the margin difference is small.
