What we stock for this range

Clutch kits, clutch discs and pressure plates, release bearings, CV joints, drive shafts, axles and gearbox mounts. Clutch kits should always be ordered complete — disc, pressure plate and release bearing — because partial replacement causes a comeback within months.

Buying note: Clutch kits must be supplied complete with the release bearing. This is the most common specification error in this category and the most expensive one to put right.
