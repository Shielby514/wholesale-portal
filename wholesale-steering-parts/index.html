What we stock for this range

Steering racks, power steering pumps, tie rod ends, drag links, steering columns and clock springs. Tie rod ends and rack ends are the volume items; complete steering racks are slower-moving but high-value. We supply both new and remanufactured racks for selected applications.

Buying note: After any steering component replacement, a wheel alignment is mandatory. Remind your workshop customers of this — it prevents the comeback that would otherwise be blamed on the part.
